# Frame Drum Image-Gen Prompts

Concept visualization only; not fabrication authority. Do not infer hoop
dimensions, head tension, tuner design, or tradition-specific details from
these prompts.

## Hero Prompt

Photorealistic 3/4 product photograph of a baseline 16-inch-class frame drum engineering prototype: a shallow hardwood hoop with a natural or synthetic head stretched across one face, clean bearing edge, simple lashing or tensioning details, and a restrained modern finish. Show the drum as a repeatable baseline that could branch into bodhran, hand drum, bendir, pandeiro, or contemporary tuned variants, with a tipper and a padded hand-drum beater nearby for scale. Clean studio background, soft directional lighting, warm wood and natural head texture, 50mm lens, high detail, photorealistic.

## In-Context Prompt

Photorealistic workshop scene of the frame drum family being compared across construction methods: one steam-bent hoop, one stave-built hoop, and one CNC-laminated or turned hoop on a bench, with a player testing the finished baseline drum by hand. Include hide-prep tools, a small air pump or inner-tube tuner prototype as credited inspiration, and mallets/sticks arranged nearby. Natural light, documentary realism, culturally respectful and non-specific.

## Detail / Macro Prompt

Photorealistic macro close-up of the frame drum rim: shallow hardwood hoop, smooth bearing edge, head material wrapped over the rim, lacing or a simple tensioning element, and visible wood grain. Include a tipper partially in frame to connect the mallet/stick scope. High-detail macro, shallow depth of field, real material texture.

## Negative / Avoid

No text, logos, watermarks, fake sacred symbols, impossible lacing, extra limbs, distorted hands, generic plastic toy drum, or dimension callouts.
